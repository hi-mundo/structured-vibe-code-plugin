# Methodology and evidence

These are methodological references, not executable plugins or automatic proof.

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 defines architecture-description concepts and viewpoints: https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html
- SEI's Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method evaluates architectural decisions against quality attributes: https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/architecture-tradeoff-analysis-method-collection/
- Nancy Leveson's *Engineering a Safer World* develops system-theoretic analysis of unsafe interactions and control: https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262533690/engineering-a-safer-world/
- IEEE 29119-4 covers test techniques, including risk-based selection: https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/29119-4/10442/
- Ammann and Offutt, *Introduction to Software Testing*, grounds model-based test criteria: https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/introduction-to-software-testing/EB9CC0789AB3F6D191A3C92B9247C8B7
- IBM's Orthogonal Defect Classification provides a defect-taxonomy method: https://research.ibm.com/publications/orthogonal-defect-classification-a-concept-for-in-process-measurements
- Śliwerski, Zimmermann, and Zeller introduced SZZ for linking bug fixes to candidate inducing changes: https://doi.org/10.1145/1082983.1083147

Use these sources to structure questions and evidence. Do not claim that a method validates product intent by itself.
